About Gornoden

Professional follow-up without unnecessary complexity

Gornoden LLC is a Maine business focused on helping other businesses manage commercial receivables and address unpaid B2B accounts. Our approach is built around clear documentation, practical communication, and client control.

We do not service consumer debt. Gornoden’s AR Management and Recovery services are designed for business-to-business commercial accounts.
